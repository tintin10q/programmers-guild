## Members

In the past year it has recruited 1 member, and it currently consists of 17 members officially on [thalia.nu](https://thalia.nu/association/societies/86/), there are 31 people in the programmers guild [WhatsApp Group](https://chat.whatsapp.com/JqDyNQmetcq6CCjQCYjVA4). 

The current contact person / chairperson is [Quinten Cabo](https://thalia.nu/members/profile/2494).

The chair person is finishing his studies this academic year and will most likely leave Nijmegen.
A new chair person contact person has to be found. [Quint van Oorschot](https://thalia.nu/members/profile/2671) has stepped forward.
He is the second founder of the programming guild.

While not that many events have been organized this year each event was a great success.
It is believed that the programming guild adds value by providing a group within Thalia where people can 
be programing things together that is not a Django website.
